# Novelty Decision

Chosen title: Semantic Force Closure: Task-Legal Contact Certificates for Robotic Grasping

The strongest direction is Semantic Force Closure. It breaks the assumption that functional contact semantics can be handled outside the force-closure certificate. The central move is small but sharp: first remove every wrench generator attached to a task-forbidden role, then solve the convex-hull certificate. This changes the witness rather than merely rescoring a grasp.

The experiment is a deterministic planar contact proxy across knife handoff, mug serving, phone pickup, spray bottle use, apple picking, and scissors passing. It is intentionally narrow: the point is not a simulator leaderboard, but to show that geometric FC and semantic acceptance can disagree with SFC in common role layouts.
